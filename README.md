One original sentence pitching a bizarre startup that is also a real
business — an entry for the
[Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a?ref=qrg7fvky)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/?ref=qrg7fvky).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Executives pay convents $99 a month per inbox to answer their unanswered emails, because a reply signed by a nun gets answered faster than any assistant's.

155 characters, one sentence. Nuns answering an executive's email sounds
like a joke until the second half lands: a reply signed by a nun gets
answered, and replies are exactly what an executive pays for. Run it locally
with `python3 main.py /tmp/entry.txt` to see the exact output.
